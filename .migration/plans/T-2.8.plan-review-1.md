## Adversarial Scrutiny & Edge Cases
1. **Lifetime & Backing Storage of Pointers in `FlutterProjectArgs`:**
   - `FlutterProjectArgs` contains raw pointers: `custom_task_runners`, `asset_resolvers`, `assets_path`, `icu_data_path`, `command_line_argv`.
   - `AndroidShellHolder` stores `task_runners_`, `apk_asset_provider_`, `settings_`, `asset_resolvers_`, and `command_line_argv_` as member variables whose lifetimes outlive any `FlutterProjectArgs` instance built by it.
   - Pointers in `project_args_` remain valid for the duration of the holder's existence.
2. **Dynamic Asset Resolver Binding:**
   - On construction, `apk_asset_provider_` may be null before `Launch(...)` is called.
   - `InitializeProjectArgs()` sets `asset_resolvers = nullptr` and `asset_resolvers_count = 0`.
   - When `Launch(...)` is called with an `APKAssetProvider`, `asset_resolvers_[0]` is bound to `apk_asset_provider_->GetFlutterAssetResolver()` and `asset_resolvers_count = 1`.
3. **Ratchet Compliance:**
   - `embedder.h` is whitelisted under `ALLOWED_HEADER_PREFIXES` in `tools/android_embedder_deps.py`.
   - No internal include counts or GN dependencies are added.
