## Background & Objectives
In Stage 2:
- T-2.1 established the `platform_view_` delegate pointer seam on `PlatformViewAndroid`.
- T-2.2 (a, b, c) migrated all 25 `PlatformView` virtual overrides on `PlatformViewAndroid` to forward across the `platform_view_` seam when set.
- T-2.3 completes the connection by constructing `PlatformViewEmbedder` inside `AndroidShellHolder`'s `on_create_platform_view` callback and wiring `SetPlatformView` on `PlatformViewAndroid`.
- T-2.4 will sever `PlatformViewAndroid : PlatformView` inheritance.

Key Architectural Considerations:
1. **Include Ratchet Neutrality:** `flutter_shell_native_src` include ratchet baseline is 98. Including `flutter/shell/platform/embedder/platform_view_embedder.h` in `android_shell_holder.cc` (+1) is paired with pruning the unused `#include "flutter/lib/ui/painting/image_generator_registry.h"` (-1), keeping internal includes at exactly 98.
2. **Behavioral Invariance:** Stage 2 changes NO behavior. Native Android GPU surface creation (`android_surface_`), external view embedders (`AndroidExternalViewEmbedderWrapper`), Impeller contexts, and vsync waiters (`VsyncWaiterAndroid`) are preserved with 100% fidelity.
3. **Dispatch Table Plumbing:** `PlatformViewEmbedder` is initialized with a `PlatformDispatchTable` that routes semantics, messages, locale, font scaling, deferred libraries, engine restart, channel updates, and view focus to `jni_facade_`.
4. **Spawn Support:** Spawned shell holders in `AndroidShellHolder::Spawn` similarly construct and wire `PlatformViewEmbedder`.
