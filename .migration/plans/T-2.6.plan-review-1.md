## Adversarial Scrutiny & Edge Cases
1. **Memory Ownership & Lifetime of `FlutterMapping`:**
   - In `get_asset_callback`, `impl_->GetAsMapping(full_path)` returns `std::unique_ptr<fml::Mapping>`.
   - Releasing the raw pointer into `mapping_out->user_data` and providing `delete static_cast<fml::Mapping*>(data)` as `mapping_out->release_callback` guarantees that memory and underlying NDK `AAsset*` descriptors are safely freed upon consumer completion.
   - For failed lookups, `mapping_out` is unmodified and returns `false`.
2. **Provider Cloning:**
   - `APKAssetProvider::Clone()` creates a new instance sharing the same `impl_`.
   - The cloned instance re-initializes its `flutter_asset_resolver_` with its own user_data pointing to `impl_.get()`, ensuring that each clone's C-ABI struct is valid and safe.
3. **Ratchet Neutrality:**
   - `embedder.h` is allowed under `ALLOWED_HEADER_PREFIXES` in `tools/android_embedder_deps.py`.
   - Zero additions to internal include counters or GN dependencies.
