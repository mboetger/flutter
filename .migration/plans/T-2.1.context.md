---

## Background & Objectives
`PlatformViewAndroid` currently inherits directly from `PlatformView`.
The overarching objective of Stage 2 is to decouple `PlatformViewAndroid` from `PlatformView`, allowing `PlatformViewEmbedder` to be constructed by `AndroidShellHolder` in T-2.3 and breaking the inheritance hierarchy in T-2.4 without changing any runtime behavior.

In T-2.1:
1. Introduce a delegate pointer `platform_view_` on `PlatformViewAndroid`.
2. Provide `SetPlatformView` and `GetPlatformViewDelegate` accessors.
3. Forward one lifecycle method (`NotifyDestroyed`) across the seam to prove delegate forwarding.
4. Verify using unit tests in `platform_view_android_unittests.cc` and ensure all Stage 0 characterization tests (specifically `platform_view_android_surface_lifecycle_unittests.cc` from T-0.2) pass unmodified.
