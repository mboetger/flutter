## Adversarial Scrutiny & Edge Cases
1. **Default State Invariant:**
   - When no flag is passed, `settings.android_embedder_api` MUST be `false`.
   - Legacy paths remain 100% active and unmodified.
2. **Flag Form Flexibility:**
   - Both `--android-embedder-api` (implicit true) and `--android-embedder-api=true` must evaluate to `true`.
   - Both `--no-android-embedder-api` and `--android-embedder-api=false` must evaluate to `false`.
3. **Plumbing and Caching:**
   - Flag is cached in `PlatformViewAndroid` and `AndroidShellHolder` at initialization and never re-read mid-lifecycle.
   - Trace event is emitted at initialization: `TRACE_EVENT1("flutter", "AndroidEmbedderApiState", "enabled", ...)`.
4. **Ratchet Invariant:**
   - No new dependencies or includes added to `shell/platform/android` targets.
   - Dependencies ratchet check will pass without baseline increases.
