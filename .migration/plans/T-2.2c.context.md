## Background & Objectives
Following T-2.1 (seam establishment), T-2.2a (semantics/locale), and T-2.2b (messaging/restart/deferred), T-2.2c migrates the remaining 9 overrides of `PlatformView` in `PlatformViewAndroid` across the `platform_view_` seam:
1. `GetPlatformMessageHandler() const`
2. `SetupImpellerContext()`
3. `CreateVSyncWaiter()`
4. `CreateRenderingSurface()`
5. `CreateExternalViewEmbedder()`
6. `CreateSnapshotSurfaceProducer()`
7. `CreateResourceContext() const`
8. `ReleaseResourceContext() const`
9. `GetImpellerContext() const`

With the completion of this batch, 100% of all virtual overrides from `PlatformView` in `PlatformViewAndroid` forward to `platform_view_` when set.
This completes task **T-2.2** in `MIGRATION_LEDGER.md`, fully clearing the path for **T-2.3** (`Construct PlatformViewEmbedder in AndroidShellHolder and wire SetPlatformView`).
