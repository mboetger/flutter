# T-0.7 Plan: Renderer Availability and Surface Lifecycle Spike (Blocker B-2)

## Goal
Resolve Blocker B-2 by delivering a technical architecture document at `engine/src/flutter/docs/engine/renderer_availability.md` and Decision Record `DR-0019`. Analyze Android surface lifecycle transitions (using T-0.2 ground truth), compare against iOS `SetGpuAvailability`, establish that renderer config and surface availability are orthogonal axes, sketch the proposed `embedder.h` additions, map to Stage 1 tasks (T-1.1, T-1.2), and log iOS human review requirements per §D.9.

## Files Touched
- `engine/src/flutter/docs/engine/renderer_availability.md` (new design deliverable)
- `.migration/decisions/DR-0019.md` (new decision record)
- `.migration/HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED.md` (append iOS sign-off note per §D.9)

## Implementation Sequence
1. Enumerate Android Surface Lifecycle Transitions:
   - Ground truth from T-0.2: `NotifyCreated`, `NotifySurfaceWindowChanged`, `NotifyDestroyed`, double notifications, and in-flight raster synchronization.
   - Explain why synchronous completion before `surfaceDestroyed` return is required by Android OS contracts.
2. Analyze iOS `SetGpuAvailability`:
   - Document `GpuAvailability` enum (`kAvailable`, `kFlushAndMakeUnavailable`, `kUnavailable`) in `shell.h` and its role in preventing background Jetsam terminations.
3. Decouple Configuration from Availability:
   - Formally demonstrate that `FlutterRendererConfig` (graphics API, device, queue) is static, while surface attachment and GPU availability are dynamic, orthogonal axes.
4. Propose Shared C-ABI Surface for `embedder.h`:
   - `FlutterEngineSetGpuAvailability` (`kFlutterGpuAvailabilityAvailable`, `kFlutterGpuAvailabilityFlushAndMakeUnavailable`, `kFlutterGpuAvailabilityUnavailable`).
   - `FlutterEngineNotifySurfaceCreated` and `FlutterEngineNotifySurfaceDestroyed` for view lifecycle.
5. Map to Concrete Stage 1 Tasks:
   - Task T-1.1: `FlutterEngineSetGpuAvailability` (shared with iOS).
   - Task T-1.2: `FlutterEngineNotifySurfaceCreated` / `FlutterEngineNotifySurfaceDestroyed` (surface teardown synchronization).
6. Record `DR-0019` and log in `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED.md`.
