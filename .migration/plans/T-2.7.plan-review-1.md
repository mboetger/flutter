## Adversarial Scrutiny & Edge Cases
1. **Thread Synchronization Invariant:**
   - In the baseline, `NotifyCreated` runs `surface->SetNativeWindow` on the raster runner synchronously via `fml::AutoResetWaitableEvent`, while `InstallFirstFrameCallback` is called on the caller's thread before the latch wait.
   - `NotifyDestroyed` calls `platform_view_->NotifyDestroyed()` on the platform thread BEFORE posting `surface->TeardownOnScreenContext()` with synchronous latch wait on the raster runner.
   - `AndroidSurfaceLifecycle` strictly preserves this invocation sequence, ensuring thread affinity and raster teardown ordering cannot race.
2. **Null Surface Handling:**
   - When running in contexts without an active `AndroidSurface` (e.g., unit tests without GPU backend or headless modes), `AndroidSurfaceLifecycle` cleanly skips raster latch waits while correctly updating its internal state and invoking delegate callbacks.
3. **Ratchet Compliance:**
   - `android_surface_lifecycle.{h,cc}` uses only headers under `flutter/shell/platform/android/`, `flutter/fml/`, and `flutter/shell/platform/embedder/embedder.h`, all of which are whitelisted in `ALLOWED_HEADER_PREFIXES`. Zero new internal headers or deps are introduced.
