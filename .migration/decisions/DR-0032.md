# DR-0032: Construct PlatformViewEmbedder in AndroidShellHolder on_create_platform_view and Wire SetPlatformView

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Task:** T-2.3
- **Date:** 2026-09-16
- **Context:** Advancing Stage 2 (Refactoring & Decoupling) per `MIGRATION_LEDGER.md` §Stage 2 by constructing `PlatformViewEmbedder` within `AndroidShellHolder`'s `on_create_platform_view` callback and wiring `PlatformViewAndroid::SetPlatformView`.

---

## 1. Problem Statement
In Stage 2:
- T-2.1 introduced the `platform_view_` delegate pointer seam on `PlatformViewAndroid` and verified forwarding for `NotifyDestroyed`.
- T-2.2 (a, b, c) migrated all 25 `PlatformView` virtual overrides on `PlatformViewAndroid` to delegate forwarding across the `platform_view_` seam when non-null, falling back to legacy implementations when null.
- T-2.3 constructs `PlatformViewEmbedder` in `AndroidShellHolder::on_create_platform_view` and wires `SetPlatformView`.
- T-2.4 will sever the `PlatformViewAndroid : PlatformView` inheritance.

Governing invariant: Stage 2 changes NO behavior. All Stage 0 characterization tests must pass unmodified. No `FlutterEngine*` calls are introduced. Ratchets must remain `<= baseline`.

---

## 2. Technical Solution

1. **Constructing `PlatformViewEmbedder` in `on_create_platform_view`:**
   - In `AndroidShellHolder::AndroidShellHolder` and `AndroidShellHolder::Spawn`, `on_create_platform_view` constructs `PlatformViewAndroid` and additionally constructs a `PlatformViewEmbedder`.
   - The `PlatformViewEmbedder` is initialized with:
     - `PlatformView::Delegate&` (`shell`)
     - `const flutter::TaskRunners&` (`shell.GetTaskRunners()`)
     - `PlatformDispatchTable` populated with callbacks routing semantics, messaging, locales, font scaling, deferred libraries, engine restart, channel updates, and view focus to `jni_facade_`.
     - `EmbedderSurface` is configured cleanly without overriding the active native Android GPU surface context.
   - `platform_view_android->SetPlatformView(platform_view_embedder.get())` is called inside `on_create_platform_view`.
   - `AndroidShellHolder` owns the `std::unique_ptr<PlatformViewEmbedder> platform_view_embedder_`.

2. **Preserving Android Surface, Compositor, and VSync Paths:**
   - On `PlatformViewAndroid`, methods providing rendering surfaces (`CreateRenderingSurface`), external view embedders (`CreateExternalViewEmbedder`), Impeller contexts (`GetImpellerContext`), snapshot producers (`CreateSnapshotSurfaceProducer`), and vsync waiters (`CreateVSyncWaiter`) continue to serve the native Android implementations (`android_surface_`, `android_context_`, `AndroidExternalViewEmbedderWrapper`, `VsyncWaiterAndroid`) until T-2.7 and T-2.9 extract surface and compositor lifecycles.
   - This ensures 100% fidelity with Vulkan/OpenGL Impeller and Skia backends and all platform view composition modes (HC, TLHC, HCPP, VD).

3. **Pruning Unused Includes for Include Ratchet Neutrality:**
   - In `android_shell_holder.cc`, `#include "flutter/lib/ui/painting/image_generator_registry.h"` is completely unused and is pruned (-1 internal include).
   - `#include "flutter/shell/platform/embedder/platform_view_embedder.h"` is added (+1 internal include).
   - Net include count change in `flutter_shell_native_src` is 0 (remains at 98, within the 98 baseline limit).
   - GN dependencies remain at 26 (within 26 baseline limit).

4. **Testing and Verification:**
   - Unit tests in `android_shell_holder_unittests.cc` assert that:
     - `holder->GetPlatformView()->GetPlatformViewDelegate()` is non-null after `AndroidShellHolder` construction.
     - In `Spawn`, the spawned holder's `GetPlatformView()->GetPlatformViewDelegate()` is also non-null.
     - All characterization and shell native unittests pass unmodified.
