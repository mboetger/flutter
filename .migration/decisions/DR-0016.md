# DR-0016: GATES Block in §D.3.1 Return Contract

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Task:** T-0.15a

## Context
§D.3.1 previously did not require an implementer to show its gates passed, so "DONE" was underspecified and was read as "I finished editing"; making the evidence inline makes a premature DONE structurally impossible rather than merely forbidden.

## Decision
The implementer return-block contract is updated to explicitly require the inline recording of all executed gates and their final verbatim lines alongside the `ROLE` and `VERDICT`. Implementers must supply: `dart analyze --fatal-infos`, `dart format --set-exit-if-changed`, `dart test`, `bin/flutter test`, the outside-`dev/tools/` experiment, and a real end-to-end tool invocation.
